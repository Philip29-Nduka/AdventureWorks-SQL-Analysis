AdventureWorks SQL Project

About the Project
This is one of my practical SQL projects where I worked with the AdventureWorks database using MySQL Workbench.
The project gave me an opportunity to practise writing SQL queries and understand how data is stored and connected across different tables.
I worked on different business scenarios covering sales, customers, employees, inventory, production, and product performance. I used JOINs to bring related information together and subqueries to answer questions that required comparing data or filtering results based on certain conditions.

What I Worked On

JOINs
-Customer purchase history
-Inventory and warehouse management
-Customer and sales order analysis using RIGHT JOIN
-Employee and departmental information
- Manufacturing work orders
- Sales territory performance
- Product category analysis
- Employee sales performance

Subqueries
- Products with above-average standard costs
- Customers who have placed orders
- Customers with above-average spending
-  Customer credit and sales performance
- Products Compared With Average Standard Cost
- Products Priced Higher Than Products in a Selected Subcategory
- Sales representatives with above-average sales performance

 What I Learned

Working on this project helped me understand how different tables relate to one another, how to retrieve information from multiple tables, and how to use subqueries to solve different business questions.
I also got more practice with aggregate functions such as SUM(), AVG(), and COUNT(), as well as GROUP BY, HAVING, and ORDER BY.
This project is part of my journey in learning SQL and improving my ability to work with data.
